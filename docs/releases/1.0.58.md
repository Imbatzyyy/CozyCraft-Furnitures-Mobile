# CozyCraft Mobile 1.0.58

Refined the sofa-to-Home transition with a warm expanding bloom, a subtle outward ripple, and a gentle sofa lift/fade. Home remains stationary underneath. The single five-second sofa cycle, one-second finishing hold, and 800ms transition are unchanged.

Economy mode omits the extra ripple. Reduced-motion preferences remain respected. Only decorative transform and opacity animations were changed; no new network requests or loading delays were added.

Validation: 206 tests, TypeScript, app build, and Chromium/WebKit animation checks at phone and tablet widths. Physical-device smoothness remains a device check.

Android is a direct-install debug APK. iOS source and assets are ready for Xcode installation, not App Store or TestFlight distribution. The customer website was not changed.
