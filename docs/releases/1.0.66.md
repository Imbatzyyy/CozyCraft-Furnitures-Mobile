# CozyCraft Mobile 1.0.66

Optional four-stop welcome tour: Discover, Wishlist, Bag, and Account. Uses the supplied guide, bag and account mascot artwork plus the existing heart pose. The welcome-voucher popup now uses the supplied voucher pose. Voucher issuance and eligibility are unchanged and remain backend-controlled.

New email signups carry a pending-tour preference. First-time Google onboarding also prepares the tour; username setup and the existing voucher popup take priority. Existing accounts are not broadly re-onboarded. Account includes a replay button. Skip, Finish, Escape and native Back dismiss the tour, with per-account local persistence and a user-metadata completion preference. Offline local completion retries synchronization on reconnect or a later mount. No schema migration or reward-rule change.

Tour motion uses short transform/opacity animation, supports reduced motion and the existing economy mode, and has no continuous animation, video or backdrop blur. Artwork remains packaged locally. Scrollable cards preserve controls on short screens; the highlight follows the real navigation button geometry.

Validation: TypeScript, 229 storefront tests, native-shell tests, Chromium/WebKit tour completion and reload-persistence checks at phone and landscape sizes with extra-large text, plus Android and iOS simulator builds. Browser emulation is not a guarantee for every physical device; live provider signup and cross-device preference synchronization remain real-account checks.

Android direct-install debug APK: versionCode 67. iOS source: 1.0.66 build 54, ready for Xcode; not an App Store or TestFlight release. Website, checkout and search unchanged.
