## Behavior

COD is capped at PHP 20,000 after valid discounts and including delivery. Unsettled COD orders share the ceiling per customer. GCash is capped at PHP 100,000; larger eligible orders use card, subject to issuer/provider limits. Disabled methods remain visible with an explanation. An invalid selection is cleared instead of switching methods for the customer.

Expired or already-applied rewards are ineligible. An expiry during Review recalculates the payable total, revalidates COD, and tells the customer to return to Payment if needed. A verification dialog tied to a changed voucher/payment selection is dismissed before proceeding with stale details.

The shared backend migration `20261022010000_checkout_payment_limits` is live and guards older clients as well. It enforces final totals after atomic reward application and rolls back rejected stock/cart/reward changes.
