# CozyCraft Mobile 1.0.82 — A more personal app menu

October 15, 2026. Android versionCode **83**; iOS build **70**.

## What changed

- Replaced the sidebar's "Your home, thoughtfully connected / A place for you"
  block with the customer's saved photo (or initials), full name, email,
  available phone number, and an action to open the existing profile screen.
  Guests receive Sign in and Create account actions instead of personal fields.
- Added direct menu access to About CozyCraft, Company History, Developers,
  Contact Us, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy. Shopping/account shortcuts
  remain available; Care & support appears once under Help & information.
- Added a dedicated, app-specific company history and short role-based
  descriptions for all five developers. The approved app roster and portraits
  are preserved, including Sammuel Guill Concepcion in place of Jacob.
- Contact Us uses published business content and configured contact settings.
  Email and available phone actions use mailto/tel; order-linked help opens the
  existing Care & support screen. No business phone number was invented.
- Registration and the in-app policy screens use one reader. The bundled
  policy wording is unchanged; viewing a document never records consent.
- Information pages preserve the mounted storefront, bag, current tab, and
  shopping scroll position. Existing focus containment, Back/Escape handling,
  rapid-tap serialization, safe areas, reading sizes, and reduced-motion support
  are retained. Fixed a pale policy-summary text conflict found during visual QA.
- Public content reads are deduplicated and cached for 30 minutes. A saved
  public copy supports offline reading, requests time out after eight seconds,
  and failures offer retry. No new realtime channel or polling interval.

## Release boundaries

This is an **app-only** release. Website, shared CMS content, database schema,
payment processing, permissions, and policy-consent rules were not changed.
The APK is debug-signed for testing, not a Google Play submission. There was no
App Store/TestFlight upload, real payment, customer message, or physical-phone
installation. Prince iPhone 17 was unavailable during this run. Browser and
simulator checks are not physical-device performance or accessibility benchmarks.

Existing local Xcode/toolchain edits were preserved outside this commit. Only
the iOS version/build changes from this task are included in the project file.
The local toolchain suite (including those pre-existing edits) passed 11 tests.
