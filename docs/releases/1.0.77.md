# CozyCraft Mobile 1.0.77 — Smoother motion and interface polish

Frontend-only release. No database, Edge Function, or backend change.

## Motion

- Launch: the full sofa drawing plays on the first launch only. Returning
  launches use a compressed 1.1 s drawing and a 450 ms splash (previously about
  8.6 s from splash to Home on every cold start).
- Tabs keep their own scroll position and restore it before paint; the new tab
  no longer visibly scrolls. Tapping the active tab returns it to the top.
- Pages push in from the trailing edge and leave the same way; search and compare
  rise; checkout steps slide in the direction of travel with an animated
  stepper. Closing pages, sheets and toasts now animate out (`Presence`).
- Progress fills, the tour spotlight and the dock indicator move by transform
  instead of width/left, so they run on the compositor.
- iPhone: drag from the left edge to go back on pushed pages. Android keeps its
  system back gesture.
- Haptics through `@capacitor/haptics` 8.0.2: add to bag, save, tab change,
  pull-to-refresh threshold, Undo and order confirmation.
- Reduced motion, economy devices and backgrounded apps keep their existing
  handling; new motion follows the same rules.

## Interface

- Labelled five-tab dock with one sliding indicator on every platform.
- Toasts show a check or an information mark and offer **Undo** for removing a
  bag item, clearing the bag, and unsaving a piece.
- Skeletons replace empty states while a signed-in customer's saved pieces, bag
  and product reviews are loading (capped at 8 s, online only).
- Photos fade in once loaded; reviewer avatars show initials under the photo
  instead of a broken image.
- Product gallery: tap or the zoom button opens the full-screen zoomable viewer;
  single-photo products no longer show arrows, counter or thumbnails.
- Order confirmation: celebrating companion, drawn check and one confetti burst.
- One round back button across Category, Notifications, Profile and Checkout.
- Avatar initials use the customer's full name (for example "AR", not "a").
- Side menu uses a chevron for in-app destinations; empty review filters are
  disabled; the care launcher steps aside while scrolling down.
- Text under 10 px now has a 10 px floor.
- Fixed a 320 px page-level horizontal overflow caused by the benefit bar; it
  now shows only whole messages. Bag controls no longer run together at 320 px.

## Verification

- Storefront: typecheck, 309 unit tests. Angular shell: 19 tests, lint.
- QA fixture audits in Chromium and WebKit, including the 160-layout design
  audit on iOS and Android treatments (320–1280 px, four text sizes).
- New offline `?shell` QA mode runs the whole storefront on sample data.
- Xcode 27.0 unsigned simulator build succeeded and the app launched on an
  iOS 27 simulator. Android `assembleDebug` succeeded (versionCode 78).
- The full-app audits (`app-navigation`, `*-onboarding`, `overall-reliability`,
  `shopping-notifications`) time out at boot on both 1.0.76 and 1.0.77; their
  intercepted backend needs updating separately.
