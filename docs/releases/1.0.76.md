# CozyCraft Mobile 1.0.76 — Thoughtfully timed shopping notifications

Opt-in offers, cart reminders and wishlist reminders, with guarded account-bound
vouchers and a small, server-enforced discount allocation. Website UI is unchanged.

## Included

- Three independent, default-off preferences in My Profile. OS permission is
  separate; settings have confirmed-save, retry and rapid-tap handling.
- Cart eligibility after 24–48 hours; wishlist after 5–7 days. At most two
  provider-accepted marketing messages per rolling week, at least 48 hours apart,
  between 10 AM and 7 PM in the customer's stored timezone.
- Cozy Surprise: **PHP 100 off PHP 10,000 merchandise**, 48-hour validity,
  one per account per 30 days, and **PHP 1,000 issued-value budget** across both
  the Manila calendar month and rolling 30 days. No automatic limit increases.
  Unused, expired and cancelled vouchers still consume the allocation.
- Existing atomic checkout enforces ownership, minimum spend, expiry, single
  use and one Home Circle reward per order. This is a limited pilot, not a profit
  guarantee; product costs and expenses have not been supplied.
- New reminder types route to the bag, wishlist or exact offer. Cold-start taps
  wait for the storefront and account setup/tutorial/welcome-voucher flow.
- Responsive ivory/sage mascot offer sheet, large-text and reduced-motion
  support, keyboard/native Back dismissal and guarded duplicate notification taps.
- Database-backed queue with bounded retries, leases, expiry and fresh
  inventory/consent/purchase checks. No phone-side recurring polling.
- New campaigns require an updated shopping-compatible device registration.
  Existing order alerts remain separate. Invalid-token cleanup corrected to
  match the live database. New campaigns exclude unknown-platform registrations;
  existing alerts retain the legacy token routing behavior.

This follows the existing GitHub/debug-APK distribution, not a Play Store,
TestFlight or App Store submission. Install the Android APK to update Android;
rebuild/run the updated project in Xcode to update iPhone. No physical device
was installed or modified by this release task.

After updating, choose My Profile → Offers and surprises / Cart reminders /
Wishlist reminders and allow notifications at OS level. Notifications are
eligibility-based, not immediate promises. See `docs/SHOPPING-NOTIFICATIONS.md`
for pause controls, actual accounting scope, provider limits and device checks.
